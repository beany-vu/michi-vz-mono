"Play through the years" (cumulative timeline) - renderer check

Manual visual QA for the opt-in timeline prop across the SVG, canvas, and WebGPU renderers (not part of the automated verify-playground self-test, and NOT published docs - this loads the freshly built packages/wc/dist bundle directly). Every chart below has timeline: {"{ autoplay: true, loop: true }"} set, so each card sweeps through its years on its own as soon as it mounts (properties are assigned BEFORE the element is appended to the DOM, same as the docs' own TimelinePlayDemo.vue - assigning them after connecting a Lit element can race its first render and miss the config). SVG and canvas should visibly reveal marks year by year; WebGPU (real GPU when your browser supports it, else it silently falls back to the canvas-2D stopgap) is EXPECTED to show the full frame immediately with no sweep and no play/scrubber control underneath - that is by design (mirrors progressiveDraw's existing "no reveal on webgpu" behaviour), not a bug. The readout under each card prints the EFFECTIVE renderer (getContext().renderer) and the headless timeline controller's state (getTimeline()).
